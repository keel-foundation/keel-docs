# Export

**Status:** specified, not implemented.

Export is a creator right, not a support favor. An export bundle must include:

- original media and renditions where retained;
- metadata, captions, thumbnails, and licenses;
- subscriber relationships subject to privacy law and subscriber consent;
- comments and analytics;
- moderation records and Statements of Reasons;
- payout history and tax documents;
- hashes and a manifest so the bundle can be imported elsewhere.

Export must be available before, during, and after an enforcement action. It must not be conditioned on silence, waiver, or deletion of an appeal.

The current demo does not implement creator export. The audit ledger has a separate JSONL/CSV export endpoint because the ledger is the first trust primitive.

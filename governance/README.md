## Governance status

The Project is in transition. The Founder and initial Maintainers adopt an interim charter, constitute an Interim Board and Interim Creators' Council, and publish a thirty-day comment period before final ratification. The first elected Creators' Council is seated within six months of the earlier of 1,000 verified creators or twelve months after the first public alpha.

The Founder's Seat and Mission Veto are transitional and sunset ten years after ratification unless extended by a Registered Creator referendum. They do not include the power to decide individual moderation or appeal cases.

## Reporting a concern

Use the repository issue tracker for public policy questions. For a moderation or appeal concern, use the appeal route in the Statement of Reasons. For a security concern, use the affected repository's Security tab.

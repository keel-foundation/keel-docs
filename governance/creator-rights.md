# Creator rights

This is a public summary of the Creator Covenant. The full Constitution remains private until ratification; this summary is the public commitment that creators can hold the Project to now.

## The rights

1. **You own your work.** Your content, metadata, and audience data remain yours. The Project receives only the license needed to host, transmit, display, promote, and operate the service.
2. **You get a specific reason.** Every enforcement action identifies the policy clause, the content or pattern at issue, the time, the decision-maker, the role of automation, the evidence, the appeal route and deadline, and the conditions for restoration.
3. **A human decides before irreversible harm.** Automation may flag, triage, and apply temporary protective holds. A human with authority to reverse must confirm termination, permanent demonetization, or material suppression.
4. **You get a real appeal.** A reviewer independent of the original decision decides the appeal, sees the full record, and gives a written answer. Appeals are not decided solely by automation.
5. **The punishment fits the conduct.** Penalties follow the published proportionality matrix. Channel-level penalties require channel-level conduct.
6. **Your earnings are protected during a dispute.** Earnings are held back, not clawed back. A reversed action restores monetization and credits attributable earnings where practicable.
7. **You can leave.** Export covers media, metadata, captions, thumbnails, analytics, moderation records, payout history, and the audience relationship where lawful. Export is not conditioned on silence or waiver.
8. **You can see the error rates.** Enforcement volumes, automation rates, appeal volumes, reversal rates, and decision times are published quarterly.
9. **Your data is not the price of participation.** Moderation does not require mass surveillance. Data is minimized and purpose-limited.
10. **The rules apply equally.** Enforcement outcomes are audited for disparate impact and the results are published.
11. **You can organize and speak.** Retaliation for protected advocacy is prohibited.
12. **You are protected from abuse.** Harassment, doxxing, threats, piracy, impersonation, spam, scams, and fraud are treated as serious harms.
13. **Your work is protected.** Content matching, notice-and-takedown, counter-notice, and repeat-infringer systems are part of the platform, not an optional add-on.
14. **Your payouts are visible and on time.** A published schedule, an inspectable double-entry ledger, clear holdback rules, timely tax documents, and protection from arbitrary withholding.
15. **Your account is secured.** MFA, session control, login alerts, and human-led recovery. A hacked channel is a victim, not a voluntary violator.

## What this does not mean

These rights do not create an unmoderated platform. Spam, scams, fraud, piracy, impersonation, harassment, CSAM, terrorism, and legal violations are enforced. The point is that enforcement must be specific, proportionate, reviewable, and appealable.

## Status

The Project is pre-alpha. The current public artifacts are a local appeal demo in `keel-dev` and a
fully built preview UI in `keel-web` (feed, watch, channels, search, library, creator studio, and
moderation console), backed by local preview data and a PostgreSQL core read API. Upload,
transcoding, HLS, payouts, federation, content protection, and the production moderation console
are not implemented yet. The demos are honest about their synthetic classifier, local-only ledger, and dev-only auth;
do not treat them as the production system.
